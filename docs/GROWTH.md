# WealthOS Growth System

WealthOS should be built as both a product and a customer-acquisition machine.

## North-star metric

**Weekly Activated Wealth Plans (WAWP)** — users who complete a profile, receive an opportunity plan, and activate at least one action.

Supporting metrics: qualified visitors, profile completion, plan generation, action activation, week-1 retention, paid conversion, customer acquisition cost, organic traffic, and referral rate.

## Acquisition flywheel

1. Publish genuinely useful financial, career, and business research.
2. Turn research topics into free interactive tools and calculators.
3. Capture visitors with a clear Wealth Plan CTA.
4. Convert completed plans into actionable execution workflows.
5. Ask activated users for referrals and testimonials with permission.
6. Use anonymized aggregate insights to produce better research.
7. Repeat across high-intent search topics.

## Initial channels

### Organic and AI search
- Build topic clusters around income growth, emergency funds, business acquisition, career leverage, side-business evaluation, and financial organization.
- Prioritize original analysis, calculators, datasets, comparisons, and first-party insights over commodity AI articles.
- Add structured metadata, internal linking, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots, and strong social previews.
- Create high-intent pages that naturally lead into Wealth Plan workflows.

### Social distribution
- LinkedIn: career leverage, income growth, business acquisition, and professional wealth strategy.
- X: concise research findings, opportunity signals, charts, and product updates.
- Short-form video: explain one concept and direct viewers to a free calculator or Wealth Plan.

### Partnerships
Target career coaches, accountants and tax professionals, business brokers, financial educators, small-business communities, entrepreneurship newsletters, employers, and professional organizations. Start with co-created education and referral relationships rather than regulated advice.

### Direct outreach
Build a permission-aware outreach system for qualified partners and potential customers. Use Outlook for approved outbound communication only after the user has explicitly connected the account and approved the workflow.

Suggested sequences: partner introduction, research collaboration, beta invitation, referral partnership, and re-engagement.

Every outreach contact should have source, consent/status, segment, last contact, next action, and outcome.

## Conversion architecture

Primary CTA: **Build My Wealth Plan**

Secondary CTA: **Explore Opportunity Intelligence**

Track the funnel: Visitor → profile start → profile completion → plan generated → action activated → return visit → subscription.

## Growth experiments

Run small measurable experiments rather than guessing: calculator vs. article landing page, short vs. long onboarding, personalized CTA copy, weekly intelligence email opt-in, referral incentive, partner co-marketing page, opportunity alert signup, and public research reports.

Each experiment records hypothesis, audience, variant, metric, start/end date, and decision.

## Trust requirements

WealthOS must clearly distinguish education and organization from regulated financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Avoid promises of returns or guaranteed financial outcomes. Use professional referral paths for complex regulated decisions.

## Product-led growth features

- shareable Wealth Plan summary
- public calculators
- opportunity watchlists
- weekly intelligence digest
- referral links
- invite-a-partner workflow
- educational research hub
- permissioned testimonials
- anonymized benchmark reports when sufficient data exists

## Outreach operating system

The product should eventually maintain a CRM-like pipeline:

Lead → Qualified → Contacted → Replied → Meeting → Trial → Activated → Paid → Referral

Integrate Outlook Mail and Calendar so approved outreach can create messages, track replies, and schedule meetings through Microsoft Graph. Microsoft Graph supports Outlook mail and calendar operations through delegated OAuth permissions.

## First growth target

Do not optimize for vanity traffic first. Optimize for a cohort of highly engaged users who complete a plan and execute an action. Their behavior should determine which acquisition channels and product workflows get scaled.
